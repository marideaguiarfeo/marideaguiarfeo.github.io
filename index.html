About

A little about me

Hi there! My name is Mariana de Aguiar Féo, but I go by Mari! I’m a rising junior at Carleton College in Northfield, Minnesota double majoring in Physics and Studio Art. I was born and raised between the cities of Teresópolis and Rio de Janeiro in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil and came to the U.S. to pursue my dream of becoming an Astrophysicist! In the meantime, I also keep exploring my passion for art through painting, photography and occasional ventures into other artistic fields.

In Astrophysics, my main interests are in transient objects and their stellar environments. I’m also very interested in stellar populations and how they relate to their host galaxy environment. Right now I’m involved in two separate projects within these topics, one exploring Type IIP Supernovae environments and the other looking into Chemical Dynamics of the Milky Way. I am also very passionate about physics education and outreach and spend a lot of my time getting involved in the physics and astrophysics community. You can read more about all these projects here.

As an artist, I do mainly photography and painting (in a variety of mediums) and often get inspiration for my subjects from other interests, like Astrophysics (surprise, surprise), dance, travel, and music.
